## Brand Gift Cards

Instantly send brand gift cards to recipients' inboxes. Choose from over 8,000 top global brands—Amazon, Walmart, and everything in between.

### Best fit if you want to:

- Rapidly distribute gift cards within minutes
- Offer hassle-free redemption with specific brand gift cards (no login or registration required)
- Generate a gift card for yourself, shareable via any channel

<Tip>
  You can also use Xoxoday's Rewards APIs to procure vouchers and front-end the communication yourself. [Learn more about the Rewards API](/api/rewards).
</Tip>

## Step-by-step guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Gift Cards">
    Click on the **Gift Cards** section under **Send Rewards**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter recipient and voucher details">
    Fill in the following information:

    **Recipient Details**

    - Email address
    - Phone number

    **Voucher Details**

    - Select voucher country
    - Select voucher brand
    - Enter a value within the brand's available denomination range
    - Select quantity (up to 999 gift cards per batch)

    Click **Add Recipient** when done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure advanced settings (optional)">
    - Add a PO number if needed
    - Add a personal message to include in the recipient's email (applies to all recipients in the batch, unless overridden in a CSV file for bulk sends)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize reward SMS (optional)">
    Select the **Reward SMS** tab and add your desired message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and send">
    Review the details and click to send the gift voucher.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resend gift cards

To resend rewards to existing contacts or in bulk:

1. Navigate to **Reports** \> **Gift Cards**
2. Select the contacts you want to resend rewards to
3. Click the three dots next to the selected contacts and select **Resend**
4. Confirm the action by clicking **Yes, Proceed** in the pop-up
