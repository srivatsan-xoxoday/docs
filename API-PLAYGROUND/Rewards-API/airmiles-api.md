### 💰 Earn & Redeem Airmiles Effortlessly

Allow users to earn miles on bookings, purchases, or partner transactions, and redeem them instantly for flights, upgrades, or exclusive rewards.

Connect to multiple airline loyalty programs and travel partners through one unified API, simplifying integration and user experience.

### 📊 Real-Time Balance & History

Provide users with real-time visibility into their miles balance, recent transactions, and redemption history.

### 🧭 Flexible Redemption Options

Enable users to redeem miles for a variety of categories, from flights and hotels to gift cards and experiences.

### 🔐 Secure & Seamless Integration

Built with enterprise-grade security and flexible APIs to integrate easily into any booking engine, wallet, or loyalty platform.
