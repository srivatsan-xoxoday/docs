Empower your users to recharge their mobile phones instantly — anytime, across the globe. The Mobile Top-up API enables seamless prepaid recharges across major telecom operators in multiple countries.

Whether your users are gifting a recharge, managing international travel needs, or simply topping up their own number, make connectivity convenient, fast, and borderless.

## 🔑 Key Features of the Mobile Top-up API:

### 🌍 Global Operator Coverage

Support for a wide range of telecom operators across countries — from Airtel in India to AT&T in the USA.

### ⚡ Instant Recharge

Enable real-time recharge with instant confirmation — fully digital and seamless.

### 🔍 Easy Plan Discovery

Allow users to browse recharge plans based on amount, validity, and benefits — for prepaid needs.

## 🚀 Benefits of Integrating the Mobile Top-up API:

### 🔄 Always-On Connectivity

Give users a fast, reliable way to stay connected — whether at home or while traveling.

### 🎁 Enable Gifting Use Cases

Let users top up loved ones’ numbers in different countries — a thoughtful and practical reward or gift.

### 💼 Ideal for Employee Benefits & Rewards

Use in loyalty programs, employee benefits, or campaign rewards — flexible and high-utility.

### 🧩 Plug-and-Play Integration

Easily integrate with your existing platform — flexible APIs that adapt to your frontend and backend.

### ⏱️ Save Time with Automation

Say goodbye to manual recharge handling — the API processes everything end-to-end in real-time.
