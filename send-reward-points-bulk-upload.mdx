### **🚧 Important**

Users must be registered on Plum to receive points; if not, they will receive a registration link to set up their account.

## **Send Reward Points to Recipients**

**Step 1:** Navigate to **Send Rewards \> Reward Points** in the main menu.

**Step 2:** Select the **Send in Bulk** option. To efficiently reward a large group of recipients, upload the CSV file by clicking the option provided.

### **👍 Pro tip**

Download the available template directly from the dashboard for your convenience.

**Step 3:** After uploading the file, click on **Next** to move ahead.

**Step 4:** Customize the marketplace.

**Step 5:** Customize the communication for email and SMS.

**Step 6:** Once done, click on **Send Rewards.Resend Reward Points in Bulk**

Steps to Resend Rewards:

- Navigate to **Reports \> Reward Points.**
- **Select Contacts:** Choose the contacts you want to resend the rewards.
- **Resend Option:** Click the **three dots** next to the selected contacts, then click **'Resend'.**
- Confirm Action: A confirmation pop-up will appear. Click '**Yes, Proceed**' to resend the rewards.
