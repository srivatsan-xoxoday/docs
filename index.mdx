## How Plum works

Plum gives admins a central dashboard to fund a wallet, create campaigns, and distribute rewards at scale. Recipients receive an email or SMS with a unique reward link or code they can redeem at a global storefront with thousands of brands.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and fund your wallet">
    Create your Plum account and add funds to your company wallet to start sending rewards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a campaign">
    Configure a Reward Link or Reward Code campaign with custom branding, denominations, and recipient communication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your tools">
    Integrate Plum with your CRM, HRMS, or survey platform to automate reward delivery based on triggers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and optimize">
    Monitor performance with detailed reports for API usage, reward links, reward codes, and payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>
