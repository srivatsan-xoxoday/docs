To start rewarding through your Plum account, you’ll first need to add funds.  
These funds act as your account balance, from which the cost of rewards (gift cards, merchandise, experiences, etc.) is deducted in real time whenever you place an order.

Having funds in your account ensures seamless order processing without order failures.
