Every reward link is mapped with a campaign, a campaign is a planned reward initiative in Plum that lets you define what rewards to offer, who receives them, and how they are delivered. It helps you organize and personalize reward distribution—whether through Reward Links or Reward Codes—making the experience more engaging for your audience.

Please follow below steps to create your first Reward Link Campaign: 

1. Log in to your Plum Admin Dashboard: http://stagingstores.xoxoday.com/admin/login
2. Navigate to the Reward Link section.
3. Click on Create New Campaign.
4. Enter a name for your campaign.
5. Click Customize to select the products you want to include.
6. Choose the country, enter the denomination, and click Save and Proceed.
7. Select the products and click Save.
8. Go to "Continue to customize Email".
9. Edit the email or SMS template as needed.
10. Adjust the background color to match your brand.
11. That’s it! 🎉 Your first Reward Link campaign is ready.
