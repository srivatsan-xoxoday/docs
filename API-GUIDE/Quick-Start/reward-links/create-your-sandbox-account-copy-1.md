Follow these steps to set up your sandbox account (If your sandbox account is already created, please move to the next step: [Here](https://dash.readme.com/project/xoxoday/v1.0/docs/create-your-api-key)):

1. Visit stagingstores.xoxoday.com/admin/accounts/signup
2. Enter your name and email address
3. Click Continue to create your admin account
4. Verify your email using the OTP sent to your inbox
5. Set a password for your account
6. Provide your region and number of employees


That’s it! Your sandbox account is now created and ready to use.

Move on to the next section to create your API key.
