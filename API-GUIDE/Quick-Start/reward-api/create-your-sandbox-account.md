Follow these steps to set up your sandbox account (If your sandbox account is already created, please move to the next step: [Here](https://dash.readme.com/project/xoxoday/v1.0/docs/create-your-api-key)):

1. Visit stagingstores.xoxoday.com/admin/accounts/signup
2. Enter your name and email address

   <img src="https://files.readme.io/8af6e4f5c513c7f5a47d8953d7e2bd288b273af893599f7c687fa9dfc1aa5016-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.06.46_PM.png" alt="" align="left" width="50% "/>
3. Click Continue to create your admin account

   <img src="https://files.readme.io/50eb15156a3666375c7c751eb60f7c2ff559c8a1c30fa0c8fa9b11621c9bb03f-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.07.29_PM.png" alt="" align="left" width="50% "/>
4. Verify your email using the OTP sent to your inbox

   <img src="https://files.readme.io/e373a7ae9aa3faf580040bd51d47f8a2a332a67b5e4e68c803a0e4dbed9bbdbe-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.07.47_PM.png" alt="" align="left" width="50% "/>
5. Set a password for your account

   <img src="https://files.readme.io/795edf23cb01778fce2c33841627086dd14a90ebbfc36102611b76eb0661a749-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.08.15_PM.png" alt="" align="left" width="50% "/>
6. Provide your region and number of employees
