Follow these steps to set up your sandbox account (If your sandbox account is already created, please move to the next step: [Here](https://dash.readme.com/project/xoxoday/v1.0/docs/create-your-api-key)):

## Create Admin Account

1. Visit: https://canvas.xoxoday.com/admin/accounts/signup

2. Enter your name and email address.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/8af6e4f5c513c7f5a47d8953d7e2bd288b273af893599f7c687fa9dfc1aa5016-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.06.46_PM.png)

3. Click **Continue** to create your admin account.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/50eb15156a3666375c7c751eb60f7c2ff559c8a1c30fa0c8fa9b11621c9bb03f-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.07.29_PM.png)

4. Verify your email using the OTP sent to your inbox.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/e373a7ae9aa3faf580040bd51d47f8a2a332a67b5e4e68c803a0e4dbed9bbdbe-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.07.47_PM.png)

5. Set a password for your account.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/795edf23cb01778fce2c33841627086dd14a90ebbfc36102611b76eb0661a749-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.08.15_PM.png)

6. Provide your region and number of employees.

   ![](https://files.readme.io/b295e9db2487c143ac4cae02d5a131f37364af3eefda9472040733e82c289757-Screenshot_2025-08-17_at_4.08.30_PM.png)

That’s it! Your sandbox account is now created and ready to use.

Move on to the next section to create your API key.
