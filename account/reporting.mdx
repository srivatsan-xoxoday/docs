Plum's reporting section gives you visibility into every layer of your rewards program—from API transaction logs and campaign delivery to wallet movements and integration health. All reports are accessible from the **Reports** item in the main dashboard navigation, and most can be filtered, column-customized, and downloaded as a spreadsheet.

<Tip>
  Review your reports regularly to identify low redemption rates, delivery failures, or budget variances early. Catching these signals quickly lets you optimize campaigns before they end.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Super Admins can access all report types. Admins can only view reports for the rewards they personally sent. Company-level financial reports are restricted to Super Admins.
</Note>

## Rewards API report (gift cards)

The Rewards API report covers every gift card order processed through the Plum API. Use it to verify order fulfillment, audit recipient delivery, and investigate failed transactions.

### Access the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Click **Reports** in the main dashboard navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Gift Cards">
    Within the Reports section, click **Gift Card**. Use the **Sent Date UTC** selector to choose your date range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review transaction data">
    The report displays the following fields for each order:

    - Order ID
    - Product Name and Product ID
    - Recipient Email
    - Currency and Denomination
    - Date with Timestamp
    - Amount Charged
    - Status
    - Actions
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Filter and customize columns

- Click the **filter icon** to narrow results by Order ID, Recipient Email, Sent Date, Delivered Date, Status, PO Number, or Tag.
- Click the **Columns icon** (|||) to show or hide specific columns.
- Click **Download** in the top-right corner to export the report.

## Reward code report

The Reward Code report shows every code generated and sent through your campaigns, along with delivery and redemption status for each recipient.

### Access the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Click **Reports** in the main dashboard navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Reward Code">
    Click the **Reward Code** tab. The report shows Unique ID, Payment Reference ID, Recipient Name, Recipient Email, and other transaction details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report">
    Click **Download** in the top-right corner to export. Use **Sent Date UTC** to set your date range before exporting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Track email and SMS delivery activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    In the Reward Code report, click the **three dots icon** next to the transaction you want to inspect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the activity timeline">
    Click **View Details**, then use the dropdown to select **Activity Timeline**. This shows the full Email and SMS delivery history for that code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Filter and customize columns

- Click the **filter icon** to apply filters across the report.
- Click the **Columns icon** (|||) to configure which columns are visible.

## Reward link report

The Reward Link report tracks the lifecycle of each reward link—from generation and delivery through to redemption.

### Access the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports > Reward Link">
    Click **Reports** in the main dashboard navigation, then click **Reward Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review link data">
    The report includes Recipient Name, Recipient Email, date of delivery, campaign details, and redemption status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a date range and download">
    Click **Sent Date (UTC)** to choose a time range, then click the **Download** button to export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Filter options

Use the filter icon to narrow the report by:

- Unique ID
- Payment Reference ID
- Recipient Name or Phone
- Sent Date (UTC) and Redemption Date (UTC)
- Status
- Campaign
- Batch ID

### Manage columns

Click the **Columns icon** (|||) to check or uncheck the columns you want to display.

## Reward points report

The Reward Points report covers all point-based transactions. It lives within the wallet history section and supports the same filtering and download capabilities as other reports.

### Access the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Reports">
    Click **Reports** in the main dashboard navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Reward Points">
    Click **Reward Points** (found under **Wallet History**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and download">
    Use filters for Recipient Name, Email, Batch ID, Phone Number, or date range. Click **Download** to export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Track email and SMS delivery activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    Navigate to **Reports \> Wallet History \> Reward Points**, then click the **three dots icon** next to the transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the activity timeline">
    Click **Take Action**, then select **Activity Timeline** from the dropdown to see per-recipient Email and SMS delivery logs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Integration report

The Integration report gives you visibility into how Plum is syncing data with your connected external tools (CRM, HRMS, survey platforms, and others).

Use this report to:

- Check the current sync status of each integration.
- Identify records that failed to sync and understand why.
- Confirm that reward triggers fired correctly from your connected platform.

To access it, click **Reports** in the main navigation and select **Integrations**.

## Payment and wallet report

The Payment and Wallet report provides a full audit trail of all financial activity on your account, including wallet recharges, reward spend, and current balance.

### Access the report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Wallet section">
    Click the **Wallet icon** in the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View transaction history">
    Click **Transaction History** to open the payment report view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review payment details">
    Each entry shows the invoice date, transaction amount, recharge source, current balance, and payment status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the report">
    Click **Download** in the top-right corner. You can also export to Excel and have it sent to your email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Filter the payment report

Use the filter controls to narrow transactions by date range, transaction type, or payment status. This is particularly useful when reconciling a specific billing period or investigating an individual transaction.

<Info>
  Super Admins can access all payment and wallet reports. Admins only see activity related to rewards they personally sent. Company-level balance and recharge history is visible to Super Admins only.
</Info>
