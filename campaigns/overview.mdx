Campaigns are Plum's primary tool for distributing rewards to groups of recipients. Instead of sending rewards one at a time, you configure a campaign once—choosing the reward catalog, denomination, branding, and recipient messaging—then reuse it every time you need to send that type of reward. Super Admins and Admins can create and manage campaigns from **Settings \> Campaigns**.

## Two campaign types

Plum supports two campaign types, each suited to different distribution scenarios.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Reward Link" icon="link" href="/campaigns/reward-link-campaign">
    Generate a shareable link that recipients click to open a branded storefront and choose their own reward. Best when you want to give recipients freedom of choice and send rewards via email or SMS.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Reward Code" icon="ticket" href="/campaigns/reward-code-campaign">
    Generate unique, pre-created codes that recipients enter on a custom landing page to access the storefront. Best when you want to distribute codes offline, through a third-party channel, or in bulk.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## When to use each type

| Scenario | Reward Link | Reward Code |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Send via Plum email or SMS | Yes | Yes |
| Distribute through a third-party channel (e.g., print, app) | No | Yes |
| Let recipients pick their own reward | Yes | Yes |
| Pre-generate codes for batch distribution | No | Yes |
| Custom branded landing page before storefront | No | Yes |
| Recipient clicks a single link to redeem | Yes | No |

## What you can customize

Both campaign types let you tailor the experience for your recipients:

- **Catalog** — Restrict the storefront to specific countries, brands, or categories, and set the reward denomination and currency.
- **Email template** — Customize the subject line, logo, banner image, greeting, and message body.
- **SMS template** — Configure a short message that accompanies the reward delivery.
- **Landing page** (Reward Code only) — Add a custom page recipients see before they reach the storefront.

<Tip>
  You can also customize email and SMS content at the time you send individual reward links, without changing the campaign template. This is useful when you need a one-off message for a specific occasion.
</Tip>
