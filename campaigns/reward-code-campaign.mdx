---
title: "Reward Code Campaign"
description: "How to set up a Reward Code campaign in Plum, generate shareable reward codes, customize the landing page, and reach recipients."
---

A Reward Code campaign generates unique codes that recipients enter on a custom landing page to access the reward storefront. Unlike a Reward Link—which sends recipients directly to the storefront via a clickable link—a Reward Code adds an intermediate step that lets you distribute codes through any channel, including print, in-app messages, or third-party platforms.

<Note>
  Reward Codes are ideal when you need to hand out rewards through a channel outside of Plum's built-in email or SMS delivery, or when you want to pre-generate a batch of codes for an event or promotion.
</Note>

Follow the steps below to create a Reward Code campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign builder">
    Go to **Settings \> Campaigns** in your Admin Dashboard. Select the **Reward Code** tab, then click **Create Reward Code Campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your campaign">
    Give your campaign a descriptive name that reflects its purpose or audience. You can also choose which categories to include or exclude from the campaign storefront at this stage.

    A clear name—for example, "Partner Summit 2026" or "Loyalty Tier Upgrade"—makes the campaign easy to identify and manage later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize the storefront">
    Tailor the reward catalog for your recipients:

    1. Select the **country** to filter the storefront to region-appropriate brands.
    2. Choose the **brands** and **categories** you want to make available.
    3. Save your changes before moving to the next step.

    Recipients will only see the brands and categories you enable here when they redeem their code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom landing page">
    A custom landing page is the first screen recipients see when they enter their reward code—before they reach the storefront. Use it to reinforce your brand, explain the reward, or add a personal message.

    Configure the landing page content, logo, and imagery to match your campaign's look and feel. When recipients redeem a code, they'll see this page first, then proceed to the storefront to choose their reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up recipient communication">
    Configure the email that gets sent when you distribute reward codes through Plum.

    - Set the **email subject line**.
    - Upload or replace the **logo** by clicking **Edit Logo**.
    - Add or update the **banner image** by clicking **Edit/Remove Banner**.
    - Click **Edit Message** to customize the greeting, message title, and body text. Toggle the recipient's name on or off, choose a preset greeting or write your own, and adjust the content alignment.

    You can also configure an SMS message for recipients who prefer text-based delivery.

    <Tip>
      Keep your email message focused on the action you want recipients to take—entering their code. A clear subject line and a single call to action ("Enter your code to claim your reward") improve redemption rates.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Once all settings are complete, click **Create** to save and activate your Reward Code campaign.

    After the campaign is created, you can generate reward codes—either one at a time or in bulk—and distribute them to recipients through any channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generating and sharing codes

After creating your campaign, navigate to it in **Settings \> Campaigns** under the **Reward Code** tab. From the campaign detail view, you can:

- **Generate codes in bulk** — Specify how many codes you need and download them as a file for distribution.
- **Generate a single code** — Create an individual code on demand.
- **Send via Plum** — Distribute codes directly to recipients through Plum's built-in email or SMS.
