---
title: "Edit, Duplicate, Disable, and Delete Campaigns"
description: "Manage your Plum reward campaigns—update settings, duplicate for reuse, temporarily disable to pause activity, or permanently delete them."
---

After creating a campaign, you can update its settings, copy it for a similar program, pause it without losing any data, or remove it entirely. All campaign management actions are available from **Settings > Campaigns** in your Admin Dashboard. Select the **Reward Link** or **Reward Code** tab depending on the campaign type you want to manage.

## Edit a campaign

Editing lets you update a campaign's name, catalog, categories, or communication templates without creating a new campaign from scratch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Campaigns">
    Navigate to **Settings > Campaigns** and open the relevant tab (Reward Link or Reward Code).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    Find the campaign you want to update and click **Actions**, then select **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your changes">
    Update any of the following:

    - **Campaign name** — Rename it to reflect a new purpose or audience.
    - **Categories and subcategories** — Add or remove product categories from the storefront.
    - **Email template** — Update the logo, banner, greeting, or message body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** (or the equivalent confirmation button) to apply your changes. The campaign is updated immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Duplicate a campaign

Duplication copies all settings and configurations from an existing campaign into a new one, so you can run a similar program without starting from scratch.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the campaign">
    Go to **Settings > Campaigns** and select the tab for the campaign type you want to copy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    Each campaign row has a three-dot menu (⋮) on the right. Click it to see available actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duplicate Campaign">
    Click **Duplicate Campaign**. A dialog appears with a name field pre-filled as "Copy of [Original Campaign Name]".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and customize">
    Rename the campaign if needed, then click **Customize Your Communications** to review and update the email template for the new campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create** to save the duplicate. The new campaign is created in an inactive (draft) state so you can review everything before activating it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What gets duplicated:**

- Campaign settings and configuration
- Rules and catalog selections
- Email and SMS templates

<Tip>
  Before activating a duplicated campaign, review expiry dates, reward denominations, and any time-sensitive messaging to make sure they reflect the new campaign's context.
</Tip>

## Disable a campaign

Disabling pauses a campaign without deleting it. Recipients cannot redeem new reward links or codes from a disabled campaign, but you can re-enable it at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the campaign">
    Go to **Settings > Campaigns** and open the relevant tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the three-dot menu">
    Click the three-dot menu (⋮) next to the campaign you want to pause.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Disable">
    Click **Disable**, then confirm by clicking **Yes, Disable** in the pop-up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To re-enable the campaign later, return to the same menu and select **Enable**.

<Warning>
  **Disable vs. delete:** Disabling a campaign is reversible—you can enable it again whenever you need it. Deleting a campaign is permanent and cannot be undone. For Reward Code campaigns in particular, deletion is not supported; you can only disable them. If you're unsure whether you'll need a campaign again, disable it rather than delete it.
</Warning>

## Delete a campaign

Deleting permanently removes a Reward Link campaign. This action cannot be undone.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the campaign">
    Go to **Settings > Campaigns > Reward Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Actions menu">
    Click **Actions** next to the campaign you want to remove.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete and confirm">
    Choose **Delete** and confirm when prompted. The campaign is permanently removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Reward Code campaigns cannot be deleted—only disabled. If you want to stop a Reward Code campaign, use the Disable action instead.
</Warning>

## Customize reward communication

You can update the email and SMS templates for any campaign at any time through the Edit action described above. Plum also lets you customize communication settings at a platform level, giving you control over branding elements that apply across all campaigns.

**What you can customize per campaign:**

- **Logo** — Upload your company logo to appear in reward emails.
- **Banner image** — Add or replace the banner in the email header.
- **Email subject line** — Write a subject that matches the campaign's purpose.
- **Greeting and message** — Choose from preset greetings or write a custom one. Toggle whether to include the recipient's name.
- **Message body** — Add a title and body text, and set the text alignment.
- **SMS message** — Write a short, direct message for SMS delivery.

**Supported communication channels:**

- Email (transactional and promotional templates)
- SMS (short, time-sensitive messages)
- WhatsApp (interactive messages and engagement flows, where configured)

<Info>
  Custom branding—including colors, fonts, sender name, and sending domain—can be configured at the account level. Contact your Plum account team or reach out to cs@xoxoday.com to set up white-labeled communications.
</Info>
