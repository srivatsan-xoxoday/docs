## **About Reward Link**

Reward Link lets you create campaigns and send rewards easily. Share the link via any channel, and recipients can redeem rewards from a selection of top global brands.

## **Reward Link is a good fit if you want to**

- Provide recipients with one-click redemption without the hassle of account creation 👍
- Create campaigns with micro-landing pages with your hand-picked gift selections 👍
- Customize link expiry dates to match campaign needs 👍

### **🚧 Pre-requisites**

You'll need to create a campaign to be able to send rewards to recipients. [<u>Click here</u>](https://help.xoxoday.com/en/articles/9388891-customize-reward-communication) to learn how.\
**Send a Reward Link to an individual**

Here are the quick steps to send one at a time:

**Step 1**: Navigate to **Send Rewards \> Reward Link \> Select Campaigns.** Use the drop-down to select the desired campaign name.

**Step 2:** Once done, choose "**Select Individually"** under the Add Recipients page.

**Step 3:** Add the recipient's name and email address. Using advanced settings, you can customize email messages and notes for internal reference.

**Step 4:** Now, you can add more recipients manually to move ahead.

**Step 5:** Set an expiry (default is 12 months) and customize email and SMS messages as needed.

**Step 6:** Click the “**Send Reward**” button at the bottom of the page to send the reward link.
