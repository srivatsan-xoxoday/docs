Plum by Xoxoday is a rewards and incentives platform that lets you send gift cards, reward links, and reward points to anyone — customers, employees, survey respondents, sales prospects, and more. As an admin, you fund a wallet, build campaigns, and dispatch rewards. Recipients receive a notification and redeem their reward on a branded storefront without needing a separate account with your organization.

## What you can do with Plum

Plum covers the full rewards workflow in one place:

- **Send rewards** — deliver gift cards, reward links, or reward points individually or in bulk
- **Run campaigns** — set up targeted reward campaigns with custom branding, expiry rules, and delivery templates
- **Integrate with your tools** — connect Plum to HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, SurveyMonkey, Zapier, and more to trigger rewards automatically
- **Manage your wallet** — recharge your balance, track spend, and control budgets by team or campaign
- **Report on activity** — monitor redemption rates, outstanding balances, and transaction history

## The three reward types

Plum supports three ways to deliver value to your recipients:

| Reward type | How it works | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gift cards** | Recipients choose a gift card from the storefront and receive a unique redemption code for the partner brand | Customer loyalty, employee recognition |
| **Reward links** | A single-use link lets the recipient browse the storefront and redeem their allotted value | Sales incentives, survey completion |
| **Reward points** | Points are credited to a recipient's Plum account and spent across the storefront over time | Long-running loyalty or engagement programs |

## How rewards flow

The typical journey from setup to redemption looks like this:

1. **Fund your wallet** — add balance via bank transfer, credit card, or invoice so you have funds available to send
2. **Create a campaign** — choose a reward type, set the value, customize the notification email, and define your recipient list
3. **Send rewards** — dispatch rewards manually, by CSV upload, or automatically via an integration
4. **Recipient redeems** — the recipient receives an email or SMS, clicks through to the storefront, picks a reward, and gets their code or credit

<Note>
  Rewards dispatched from a campaign draw down your wallet balance in real time. Make sure your wallet has sufficient funds before launching a campaign.
</Note>
