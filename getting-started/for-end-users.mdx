When someone sends you a reward through Plum, you receive a notification by email or SMS. You do not need to create a business account — you simply follow the link in your notification, sign in or register on the storefront, pick the gift card or voucher you want, and collect your unique redemption code. This page walks you through every step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your reward notification">
    Check your inbox (or SMS messages) for a reward notification from the organization that sent you the reward. The email subject and sender name reflect the brand of the company who sent the reward, so look for a message from them rather than from Plum directly.

    Inside the email you will see the reward value and a **Redeem now** button. Click it to open the Plum storefront.

    <Tip>
      If you cannot find the email, check your spam or promotions folder. Add the sender to your contacts to make future reward emails easier to find.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign up or log in to the storefront">
    The **Redeem now** link takes you to the Plum reward storefront. If this is your first time using Plum, you will be prompted to create an account using the email address the reward was sent to.

    - Enter your name and choose a password, then click **Create account**
    - Plum sends a short verification code to your email — enter it to confirm your address
    - If you already have a Plum account, click **Log in** and sign in with your existing credentials

    <Note>
      Your storefront account is separate from any corporate system. You only need your email address and a password of your choice.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the reward catalogue">
    Once you are signed in, the storefront displays the campaign catalogue — a collection of gift cards and vouchers available to you. The value loaded onto your account reflects the amount the sender allocated.

    Browse by category (such as Shopping, Dining, Travel, or Entertainment) or use the search bar to find a specific brand. Each card shows the brand logo, a brief description, and any terms or expiry date you should be aware of.

    <Info>
      The catalogue you see is curated by the organization that sent your reward. Not every brand available on Plum may appear in your specific catalogue.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your reward">
    Click on the gift card or voucher you want. A detail page opens showing:

    - The brand and redemption instructions
    - Supported denominations (some brands let you choose the exact amount)
    - Expiry date for the voucher code

    Choose your preferred denomination if prompted, then click **Collect voucher** (or **Add to cart** if the storefront supports a cart flow).

    <Warning>
      Review the brand's terms before confirming. Some gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged once issued. If you have remaining balance after your selection, you can return to the catalogue and redeem it against another brand.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your redemption code">
    After you confirm your selection, Plum generates a unique voucher code (and in some cases a PIN) tied to the brand you chose. This code is displayed on screen and also sent to your registered email address.

    To use your reward:

    1. Visit the brand's website or app
    2. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout
    3. Enter the voucher code in the gift card or promo code field
    4. The value is applied to your order total

    <Tip>
      Save or screenshot the code immediately after it is displayed. While Plum also emails it to you, having a local copy means you can use it even without internet access to your inbox.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common questions

**What if my reward link is expired?** Reward links have an expiry date set by the organization that sent them. If your link has expired, contact the sender directly — Plum support cannot extend or reissue links on behalf of senders.

**Can I split my reward value across multiple brands?** Yes. If your reward value is larger than a single denomination, you can return to the catalogue after your first redemption and use the remaining balance on a different brand.

**What if a voucher code does not work at checkout?** Check that you are entering the code on the correct brand's website and that the code has not already been used. If the code is valid but still not working, contact Plum support with your order reference number and the code you received.
