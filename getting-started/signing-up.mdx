title: "Signing Up"
description: "Step-by-step guide to creating and verifying your Plum account, funding your company wallet, and inviting your first admin user."
---

Getting started with Plum takes only a few minutes. You create an account, verify your organization details, add funds to your wallet, and invite any colleagues who will help you manage rewards. Once those steps are complete, you are ready to build your first campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the Plum sign-up page and enter your work email address, full name, and a password. You can also sign up using Google SSO if your organization uses Google Workspace.

    After you submit the form, Plum sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click **Verify email** to confirm your address.

    <Tip>
      Use your work email rather than a personal address. Plum uses your email domain to associate your account with your organization and to verify billing details.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your account">
    After email confirmation, Plum may ask you to complete organization-level verification before you can send rewards. This step varies by region and may include:

    - Entering your company name and registered address
    - Uploading a business registration document or proof of identity
    - Confirming your intended use case (employee rewards, customer incentives, etc.)

    Once your details are reviewed and approved, your account status changes to **Verified** and all sending features become available.

    <Info>
      Verification is typically completed within one business day. You will receive an email notification when your account is approved.
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    Before you can send any rewards, you need to add balance to your Plum wallet. Navigate to **Account** \> **Wallet** and select **Recharge wallet**.

    Choose your preferred payment method:

    - **Credit or debit card** — funds are available immediately after payment
    - **Bank transfer (wire/ACH)** — funds appear within 1–3 business days depending on your bank
    - **Invoice** — available for enterprise accounts; contact your account manager to enable this option

    Enter the amount you want to add, review the billing address shown, and confirm the transaction. Your wallet balance updates as soon as the payment clears.

    <Note>
      You can update your billing address during the recharge flow by clicking **Edit billing address** on the payment confirmation screen. Changes apply to that transaction and are saved for future recharges.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite admin users">
    If other team members will create campaigns, manage the wallet, or run reports, invite them as admin users. Go to **Account** \> **User management** and click **Invite user**.

    Enter their email address and select a role:

    - **Admin** — full access to campaigns, wallet, integrations, and reporting
    - **Finance** — can recharge the wallet and view financial reports, but cannot create campaigns
    - **Viewer** — read-only access to campaigns and reports

    The invitee receives an email with a link to set up their password and access your Plum account. Each invited user is added to the same organization account, so they share the same wallet balance and campaign history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch between accounts">
    If you belong to more than one Plum organization — for example, you manage rewards for multiple business units or clients — you can switch between them without logging out. Click your organization name in the top navigation bar to see a list of accounts you have access to, then select the one you want to work in.

    <Tip>
      Bookmark your primary account's storefront URL for quick access. Each Plum account has its own subdomain or custom domain that you can share with your recipients.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to do after setup

With your account verified and your wallet funded, you are ready to start sending rewards. Head to the [Campaigns overview](/campaigns/overview) to build your first campaign, or explore the [Integrations overview](/integrations/overview) if you want to trigger rewards automatically from your CRM or survey tool.
