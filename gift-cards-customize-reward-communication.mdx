## Customizing Email

You have the option to customize email and SMS based on your requirements.

You can customize the following elements before sending Gift Cards:

- The subject line of the email
- Headline message
- Banner image
- Personal message
- Email footer

## Customizing SMS

To customize the SMS, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Reward SMS** tab.
2. Add your message in the input field and proceed.
