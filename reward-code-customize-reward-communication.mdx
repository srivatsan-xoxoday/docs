<Tip>
  You can customize your Reward communication at the campaign level. These settings become the default communication template for the campaign. You can also customize a specific communication at the 'Send Reward' option, which will override the default campaign template message for that particular recipient(s).
</Tip>

## Customizing Email in a Reward Code Campaign

You have the option to customize the email either within the "Campaign" section or directly in the "Reward Code" section on the admin dashboard.

### Editing Email in Reward Code Section

To customize the email from the "Reward Code" section, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Reward Code section">
    Access the Reward Code section in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Email Preview">
    After configuring the campaign and uploading recipients, access the "Email Preview" section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize email elements">
    You can customize the following elements before sending the Reward code:

    - Subject line
    - Headline Message
    - Banner Image
    - Email footer

    The banner can also be deleted by clicking on "Remove Banner".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the message">
    Click on "Edit Message" and enter the message title and body. Once done, click on "Save".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit background">
    Click the pen icon to choose from available colors, or enter your own using a hex code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit background text">
    Select one of the available options or enter your own text. Then click "Save" to confirm your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customizing SMS

To customize SMS, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a Reward SMS">
    Choose the SMS template you want to customize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your message">
    Enter your message in the input field to customize the SMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Expiry (Optional)">
    Select a duration from the dropdown. Options include 1 month, 12 months, or a custom timeframe.
  </Step>
</Steps>
