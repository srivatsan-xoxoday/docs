<Info>
  For emails to not land in spam and properly open images in email services like Microsoft Outlook and Gmail, follow the steps below.
</Info>

## Microsoft Outlook Email Whitelisting Process

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Option 1: From Email Message">
    In an open message sent from a particular email address or domain, right-click on a blocked item and do one of the following:

    - Click **Add Sender to Safe Senders List**.
    - Click **Add the Domain \[@domain\] to Safe Senders List**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Option 2: From Settings">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Click File in the header">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Options from the left side menu">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Trust Centre from the left panel">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Trust Center Setting">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Uncheck 'Don't download pictures automatically in HTML email messages or RSS items.'">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Refresh and restart Outlook">
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Option 3: From Microsoft Admin">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Login to https://portal.microsoft.com">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Admin > Exchange > Mail Flow">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click + and select Bypass Spam Filtering">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Set Apply this rule if… > The sender… > domain is">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter the domain name (example: rolet.com)">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click + under specify domain to add the domain to the list">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check Stop processing more rules">
      </Step>
      <Step title="Click Save">
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      You can add multiple domain names to the same rule without creating additional global rules in Mail Flow.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Google Email Whitelisting Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Admin console > Apps > G Suite Gmail > Advanced settings">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Spam section, hover over the setting, and click Configure">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a unique name for the setting">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check 'Bypass spam filters for messages received from addresses or domains within this approved senders lists'">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Use existing or Create a new one', enter a list name, and click Create">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over the list name, click Edit, then click Add">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the domain name (example: abc.com)">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
  </Step>
</Steps>
