Plum's Zoho CRM integration extends what you can do with your CRM by adding rewards directly into your sales workflow. You can reward Leads and Contacts from a widget on their record page, and you can set up Zoho workflow rules that automatically trigger reward delivery when specific conditions are met—such as when a deal closes or a lead reaches a milestone.

## What you can do with the integration

- Send 1:1 rewards to Leads and Contacts from a widget inside Zoho CRM.
- Create and personalise reward campaigns with custom reward types, denominations, and email templates.
- Automate reward delivery using Zoho CRM Workflow Rules, without any manual processing.
- Get a unified view of all reward automation campaigns from within the Zoho integration.
- Re-engage cold leads, book more meetings, and boost referrals by including rewards in your outreach.

## Connecting Plum to Zoho CRM

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations in Plum">
    Log in to your Plum admin dashboard and click **Integrations**. Search for **Zoho CRM** and click **Connect**. You will be redirected to the Zoho Marketplace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Xoxoday Rewards app">
    In the Zoho Marketplace, find the **Xoxoday Rewards for Zoho CRM** app and click **Install**. Accept the terms and conditions and confirm the installation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once installed, the Plum Rewards widget becomes available on your Lead and Contact detail pages.

## Sending 1:1 rewards from a contact record

<Info>
  Zoho CRM users who need access to the 1:1 rewards widget must be added as a user in Plum with Admin or Super Admin access.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Lead or Contact record">
    In Zoho CRM, navigate to the Leads or Contacts module and open the record of the person you want to reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Plum Rewards widget">
    On the detail page, click **Plum Rewards** to open the reward widget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a reward campaign">
    Choose the campaign you want to use from the dropdown. Campaigns define the reward type, denomination, and catalog available to the recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next and customise the email">
    Proceed to the email customisation step. Update the **Email Subject** and **Message** to personalise the reward for this specific contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an email banner">
    Click **Email Banner** to browse banners organised by occasion—appreciation, celebration, thank you, and more. Select the banner that fits and click **Use Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview the email">
    Click **Preview your email** to see exactly how the reward email will appear to the recipient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the reward">
    Click **Send**. The contact receives a branded reward email with a unique claim link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting up automation with Zoho workflow rules

Workflow rules in Zoho CRM let you automatically send rewards whenever a record matches criteria you define—for example, when a deal is marked Won, when a new lead is added, or when a specific field is updated.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Workflow Rules">
    In Zoho CRM, go to **Setup \> Automation \> Workflow Rules**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new rule">
    Click **Create Rules**. Set the **Module** for which you want to automate rewards (for example, Deals or Leads). Add a descriptive name and description for the rule so its purpose is clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the trigger conditions">
    Define when the rule should fire. For example, to reward a sales rep when they close a deal before its closing date, configure the rule to trigger when **Deal Stage is Closed Won** and the **Closing Date** is in the future.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Plum Rewards as the action">
    Under **Instant Actions**, select **Plum Rewards (PlumrewardsbyXoxoday)**. Choose the Plum reward automation you want to associate with this rule, and give the custom action a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate the rule">
    Click **Save**. By default, rules are inactive. Click **Activate** to start running the workflow. From this point, every matching record will trigger a reward automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create reward campaigns in Plum before setting up Zoho workflow rules, so the automation is available to select in the Instant Actions step.
</Tip>

## Zoho People integration

If your organisation also uses Zoho People for HR, Plum has a separate integration for employee recognition. Administrators and reporting managers can award reward points directly from an employee profile in Zoho People. Employees then redeem their points on Plum's global catalog without a separate login—Plum handles authentication in the background via SSO.

Key capabilities of the Zoho People integration:

- Award points from any employee profile (admins and reporting managers only).
- Configure which locations and employee types are eligible for rewards.
- Employees browse and redeem from gift cards, merchandise, experiences, and vouchers.
- Points are applied automatically at checkout; no additional payment required.

To set up the Zoho People integration, go to your Plum admin dashboard, click **Integrations**, find **Zoho People**, and click **Connect**.
