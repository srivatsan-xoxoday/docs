Plum works with the tools your teams already use. Whether you run marketing campaigns in HubSpot, manage customer relationships in Salesforce, run surveys in Qualtrics, or want to trigger rewards from any app via Zapier, Plum's native integrations let you automate reward delivery without switching platforms.

## Integration categories

Plum integrations fall into four main categories based on use case and team type.

### CRM and marketing automation

Connect Plum to your CRM or marketing automation platform to send rewards directly from contact records, trigger reward delivery based on deal stages or lifecycle changes, or enrich email campaigns with unique reward links.

- **HubSpot** — Send 1:1 rewards from a contact profile, automate rewards via workflows, or distribute 1-Many reward links in bulk email campaigns.
- **Salesforce** — Install the Plum package from AppExchange, send 1:1 rewards from Lead and Contact records, and create Salesforce Flows that trigger rewards automatically.
- **Zoho CRM** — Send rewards to Leads and Contacts from the Plum widget and automate delivery using Zoho Workflow Rules.
- **ActiveCampaign** — Add Plum as an action in ActiveCampaign automations to reward contacts when they meet your defined criteria.

### Survey and research tools

Use rewards to increase survey completion rates. Plum integrates with the most widely used survey platforms to automatically send rewards when a respondent submits a survey response.

- **SurveyMonkey** — Set up public or anonymous survey automations so that every respondent who completes your survey receives a reward instantly.
- **Qualtrics** — Use the Xoxoday Workflow Extension inside Qualtrics to send rewards on survey completion, with control over approval and reward limits.
- **Typeform** — Connect Typeform to Plum via the native integration to reward new form submissions automatically.

### HRMS and people tools

Recognise and reward employees directly inside your HR platform.

- **SAP SuccessFactors** — Employees redeem their SuccessFactors points on Plum's global catalog via SSO.
- **Darwinbox** — Employees log in to Plum's storefront from the Darwinbox Recognition tile using SSO and redeem points on gift vouchers and experiences.
- **Zoho People** — Admins and managers award points from employee profiles in Zoho People; employees redeem through Plum's catalog without a separate login.

### No-code automation

Connect Plum to any app that has a Zapier integration, without writing code.

- **Zapier** — Build Zaps that trigger a Plum reward whenever something happens in another app—a form submission, a new CRM record, a completed survey, or any other event.

## Integrations report

The Plum Integrations Report in your admin dashboard gives you visibility across all active integrations. You can monitor reward delivery status, track data synchronisation, and diagnose issues from a single view.

<Note>
  Each integration has its own reporting dashboard. Navigate to **Manage Integrations** in your Plum admin panel, open the relevant integration, and review the automation detail page to see triggered rewards, redemptions, and order status.
</Note>
