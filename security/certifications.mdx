This page explains each certification, what it covers, and what it means for you as a customer.

## Certifications and frameworks

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="ISO 27001:2013">
    **What it is:** ISO 27001 is an internationally recognized standard for Information Security Management Systems (ISMS). It defines a systematic approach to managing sensitive information through a combination of policies, processes, and technical controls.

    **What Plum does:** Plum has achieved ISO 27001:2013 certification, meaning an independent auditor has verified that Plum's information security management program meets all required controls across areas such as risk management, asset classification, access control, cryptography, physical security, incident management, and business continuity.

    **What it means for you:** You can trust that Plum applies a structured, audited approach to protecting information—not just technical controls, but the organizational policies and processes that surround them. The certificate is renewed through annual surveillance audits.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOC 2 Type II">
    **What it is:** SOC 2 (Service Organization Controls 2) is a reporting framework developed by the American Institute of CPAs (AICPA). A Type II report covers a period of time (typically 12 months) and evaluates the design and operating effectiveness of controls across five Trust Service Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy.

    **What Plum does:** Plum is SOC 2 certified. The audit is conducted annually by Laika Compliance LLC, a certified third-party auditor. AWS, Plum's cloud infrastructure provider, independently holds SOC 1, SOC 2, and SOC 3 reports, adding an additional layer of assurance at the infrastructure level.

    **What it means for you:** SOC 2 Type II provides evidence that Plum's security controls were not only designed correctly but also operated effectively over the audit period. You can request the report through your account representative to review the specific controls tested.

    The SOC 2 Type I report is valid for one year from the date of issuance. Plum conducts the audit annually to maintain current certification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CSA STAR Level 1 (Cloud Security Alliance)">
    **What it is:** The Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) STAR program is the world's leading initiative for cloud security transparency. STAR Level 1 is a self-assessment in which a cloud provider completes the CSA Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire (CAIQ) and publishes the results to the public CSA STAR registry.

    **What Plum does:** Plum has completed the CSA STAR Level 1 self-assessment and is listed on the CSA STAR registry under Nreach Online Services Pvt. Ltd. (Xoxoday). The assessment covers all major CSA Cloud Controls Matrix (CCM) domains, including application security, data integrity, encryption, identity and access management, incident management, and more.

    **What it means for you:** The CSA STAR registry entry gives you free, on-demand access to Plum's answers to hundreds of security control questions mapped to industry frameworks. This promotes transparency and allows you to conduct a detailed review without requiring Plum to share confidential reports directly.

    You can view Plum's registry entry at: `cloudsecurityalliance.org/star/registry/nreach-online-services-pvt-ltd-xoxoday`
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)">
    **What it is:** GDPR is a comprehensive data protection law that applies to organizations processing personal data of individuals in the European Union. It establishes rights for data subjects and obligations for data controllers and processors.

    **What Plum does:** Plum is GDPR-compliant and has implemented all required technical and organizational measures (TOMs). This includes a Data Subject Access Rights procedure, Data Processing Agreements with all sub-processors, anonymization and pseudonymization policies, and a breach notification procedure aligned with the 72-hour GDPR notification requirement.

    **What it means for you:** If your organization operates in the EU or processes personal data of EU residents, Plum can serve as a compliant data processor. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available as part of the contract with Plum.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="India DPDP Act (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)">
    **What it is:** The DPDP Act is India's primary digital data protection law, enacted in August 2023 with rules effective from 2025. It governs the processing of digital personal data in India and establishes rights for data principals (individuals) and obligations for data fiduciaries and processors.

    **What Plum does:** Plum acts as a **Data Processor** under the DPDP Act, supporting its customers who are Data Fiduciaries. Plum ensures that only necessary personal data is collected, processes data for lawful purposes, applies reasonable security safeguards, and provides mechanisms to handle data principal rights including access, correction, and erasure.

    **What it means for you:** If your organization is based in India or processes personal data of Indian residents, Plum's compliance with the DPDP Act ensures that your use of the platform aligns with Indian regulatory requirements. A detailed compliance assessment report is available through Plum's Trust Center under NDA.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CPRA (California Privacy Rights Act)">
    **What it is:** The CPRA is a California state data privacy law that expands the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). It strengthens privacy rights for California residents and established the California Privacy Protection Agency as the lead enforcement body.

    **What Plum does:** Plum is CPRA-certified, having implemented all required privacy controls and had them audited by an external auditor. A CPRA Attestation Report is available to customers upon request. Plum does not collect data from users under 18, and does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

    **What it means for you:** If your users include California residents, Plum's CPRA certification supports your own compliance obligations. You can submit CPRA-related data requests to [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act)">
    **What it is:** HIPAA is a US federal law that sets standards for the protection of Protected Health Information (PHI). It applies to covered entities and their business associates who handle PHI.

    **What Plum does:** Plum is HIPAA-compliant for use cases that involve PHI. Controls include AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS in transit, role-based access controls with MFA, audit logging, data segregation, and documented incident response procedures. A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is available for healthcare clients.

    **What it means for you:** If your reward or incentive program involves healthcare participants or PHI, Plum can support compliant deployments. Request the HIPAA Audit Report and BAA through your account representative.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Indian IT Act 2000">
    **What it is:** The Information Technology Act, 2000 is India's primary legislation governing electronic commerce, cybercrime, and data protection prior to the DPDP Act.

    **What Plum does:** Plum is compliant with the Indian IT Act of 2000, including the relevant rules under Section 43A regarding reasonable security practices.

    **What it means for you:** Organizations operating in India can rely on Plum's compliance with foundational Indian IT law in addition to the more recent DPDP Act framework.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
