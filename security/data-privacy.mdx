Plum takes a privacy-first approach to how it collects, stores, and processes personal data. This page explains what data is collected, where it lives, how long it is retained, and what rights you have under applicable privacy regulations. If you have questions not covered here, contact [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com).

## What data Plum collects

Plum collects only the personal information necessary to deliver the rewards and incentives platform. The primary data stored in the system includes:

- **Names, email addresses, and contact numbers** of users and reward recipients.
- **Transaction and redemption history** tied to individual user profiles.
- **Employee identifiers and performance metrics** for organizations using Plum for recognition or sales incentive programs.

Plum does not collect payment card data for purposes other than billing, and does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties outside the organization. Data is collected exclusively through the Plum application platform.

## Where your data is stored

All customer data is stored on **AWS cloud infrastructure**, with primary data centers located in the **United States**. For enterprise clients with specific data residency requirements, Plum also supports hosting in **Singapore** or the **European Union**.

Plum does not store any customer data locally or on removable media. The platform operates entirely on cloud infrastructure, which means there are no physical storage devices outside of AWS's data centers.

<Info>
  Physical data center security at AWS includes strict access controls, perimeter security, and principle-of-least-privilege access for data center personnel. Learn more at the AWS Trust Center.
</Info>

## Data retention and deletion

Data is retained as long as it is needed to fulfill the contracted service or as required by applicable law. When you request deletion of your data, Plum follows the **DoD 5220.22-M ECE secure deletion standard** and provides a certificate confirming that data has been properly sanitized from all computing resources.

The standard process for data deletion is:

- Storage periods align with regulatory requirements.
- Personal data can be deleted on a formal written request with justification.
- Plum deletes the data within **30 days** of receiving the request.

Once data is purged, it is removed from all locations—backups included.

## Privacy compliance topics

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="GDPR (EU General Data Protection Regulation)">
    Plum is GDPR-compliant and has implemented all required technical and organizational measures (TOMs) to protect personally identifiable information. Key controls include:

    - **Data Subject Access Rights (DSAR) procedure** is in place, allowing data subjects to request access, correction, or erasure of their personal data.
    - **Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)** are in place with all sub-processors.
    - **Data Privacy Impact Assessments (DPIAs)** are conducted when a risk to personal data is identified.
    - **Anonymization and pseudonymization policies** are implemented for applicable data sets.
    - **Breach notification procedures** are documented and follow GDPR requirements.

    To submit a GDPR-related data subject request, email [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="India DPDP Act (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)">
    The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 governs the processing of digital personal data in India, with rules effective from 2025. The Act emphasizes user consent, purpose limitation, data accuracy, and individual rights.

    Under this framework, Plum acts as a **Data Processor**, while your organization acts as the **Data Fiduciary**. This means:

    - Only necessary personal data is collected and processed for lawful purposes.
    - Reasonable security safeguards protect all personal data.
    - Mechanisms exist to handle data principal rights, including access, correction, and erasure requests.
    - Proper breach notification procedures are followed.
    - All responsibilities and security commitments are defined in the contract between your organization and Plum.

    The complete DPDP Act compliance assessment report is available through Plum's Trust Center portal under NDA. Contact [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com) to request access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="CCPA / CPRA (California Privacy Rights Act)">
    Plum is certified under the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), which strengthens data privacy rights for California residents and expands the existing CCPA framework. Plum has implemented all required CPRA privacy controls, verified by an external auditor.

    Your rights under CPRA include:

    - The right to know what personal data is collected and how it is used.
    - The right to delete your personal data.
    - The right to correct inaccurate personal data.
    - The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data (Plum does not sell personal data).
    - The right to authorize an agent to exercise your rights on your behalf.

    Plum does not collect data from any user under 18 years of age. To submit a CCPA/CPRA request, email [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com). The CPRA Attestation report is available upon request through your Plum account representative.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tax compliance for rewards">
    When you distribute rewards through Plum, tax treatment of those rewards varies by country. Plum provides guidance on small benefit exemption thresholds and tax authority contacts for the following regions: USA, UK, Ireland, India, Poland, France, Netherlands, Norway, Spain, Germany, Denmark, Singapore, Malaysia, and Philippines.

    Key examples:

    - **USA:** Up to \$25 per employee per year is tax-free; amounts above this are subject to Federal Income Tax (22%), Social Security (7.65%), and applicable state/local taxes.
    - **UK:** Up to £50 per benefit is tax-free (trivial benefits exemption), provided it is not cash, not a reward for performance, and not contractual.
    - **India:** Gifts up to ₹5,000 per financial year are tax-exempt; the excess is taxed as a perquisite.
    - **Ireland:** Up to €500 per person per year is tax-exempt when provided as vouchers.

    <Warning>
      Tax guidance provided by Plum is for informational purposes only and does not constitute professional financial or legal advice. Always consult your tax authority or an advisor for your specific situation.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Privacy request process">
    To exercise any privacy right—including data access, correction, deletion, or portability—submit a written request to [cs@xoxoday.com](mailto:cs@xoxoday.com).

    Include the following in your request:

    - Your full name and the email address associated with your account.
    - The specific right you wish to exercise.
    - Any relevant justification (required for deletion requests).

    Plum will acknowledge your request and complete the applicable action within the timeframes required by the relevant regulation (for example, within 30 days for deletion requests). If you are acting as an authorized agent on behalf of a data subject, you must provide written permission from the data subject.
