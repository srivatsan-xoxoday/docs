The Reward Link API—also referred to as the XoxoLink API—lets you programmatically send shareable reward links to recipients. Rather than choosing a specific gift card or reward on behalf of the recipient, you send them a link that lets them browse and select their own reward from Plum's full catalog of thousands of products across brands, categories, and geographies. This choice-based approach increases recipient satisfaction and reduces the administrative overhead of managing individual voucher orders.

## What you can do with the Reward Link API

- **Send reward links**: Generate and deliver a personalized XoxoLink to any recipient via email or SMS.
- **Check link status**: Retrieve the redemption status of a sent link to see whether it has been opened, redeemed, or is still pending.
- **Resend links**: Resend a previously sent link if the recipient did not receive it or the link has expired.
- **Validate tokens**: Confirm that your access token is valid before initiating reward link operations.

## How it works

1. You call `POST /send-link` with the recipient's details, the campaign or reward configuration, and an amount.
2. Plum generates a unique XoxoLink and delivers it to the recipient.
3. The recipient opens the link and browses the Plum catalog to choose their preferred reward.
4. You can monitor redemption progress using `GET /get-link-status`.

## Supported use cases

- **Employee recognition**: Let employees choose their own reward from thousands of options.
- **Customer incentives**: Send post-purchase or referral rewards that recipients redeem at their convenience.
- **Survey and research incentives**: Distribute reward links as incentives for survey participants.
- **Event and milestone rewards**: Automate link delivery for work anniversaries, birthdays, or performance milestones.

## Available endpoints

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| POST | `/validate-token` | Validate your access token |
| POST | `/send-link` | Send a reward link to a recipient |
| GET | `/get-link-status` | Get the redemption status of a sent link |
| POST | `/resend-link` | Resend a previously sent reward link |
