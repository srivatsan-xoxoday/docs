The Plum Perks API lets you embed curated employee perks and exclusive brand offers directly into your application. Rather than directing your users to a separate rewards portal, you can fetch available offers and surface them natively inside your HR platform, employee app, or engagement tool. This creates a seamless experience where employees discover and access perks without leaving the tools they already use.

## What you can do with the Perks API

- **Authenticate**: Use `GET /access-token` to generate a Perks-specific access token scoped to the offers catalog.
- **Fetch offers**: Call `GET /fetch-offers` to retrieve the current list of available perks, deals, and exclusive brand offers available to your users.

## What Plum Perks includes

The Perks catalog includes curated, exclusive offers from brands across categories such as:

- Dining and restaurants
- Travel and accommodation
- Entertainment and streaming
- Health and wellness
- Shopping and e-commerce
- Financial services and insurance

Plum curates and updates these offers regularly, so your users always see current, relevant deals.

## Supported use cases

- **Employee benefits portals**: Embed a perks section inside your company intranet or HR platform so employees can browse offers without switching tools.
- **Engagement platforms**: Supplement your rewards program with lifestyle perks to increase platform engagement and daily active use.
- **Onboarding experiences**: Surface exclusive welcome offers to new employees as part of their onboarding flow.

## Available endpoints

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| GET | `/access-token` | Generate a Perks API access token |
| GET | `/fetch-offers` | Retrieve available perks and brand offers |
